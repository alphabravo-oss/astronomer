Connection metrics have a collector cap of 10,000 unique owners and 500 rows per page. At most 21 queries include the final empty sentinel when the cap is filled exactly. This engineering limit is ten times the 1,000-cluster extreme-lab target in `docs/plans/2026-06-13-rancher-quality-argo-master-plan.md`; it is not measured or certified fleet capacity. The collection has a ten-second read-only repeatable-read transaction and bounded rollback cleanup. Driver failure, iterator error, cancellation, timeout, oversized pages, excess rows and commit failure discard the whole observation.

The registry has the same hard 10,000-owner limit. An admitted connection beyond the metric budget receives no counter children; authentication, tunnel admission and I/O remain unchanged. `astronomer_connection_metrics_admissions_total{result="budget_dropped"}` exposes this loss. Existing owners retain counters and every readmission advances freshness. No historical tombstone map or age-based eviction is used. Captured child references can outlive retirement, but their increments cannot register a new series.

Publication preflights its final registry size under the owner lock before changing any vector or owner. Refused publication leaves the prior observation and counters intact, records `astronomer_connection_metrics_observations_total{result="owner_budget"}`, and does not refresh `astronomer_connection_metrics_last_complete_timestamp_seconds`. Other finite collection results are `complete`, `page_budget`, `canceled` and `failed`. The timestamp records the collection start of the most recent complete observation. An absent timestamp means no complete observation yet. These signals must distinguish stale/incomplete metrics from current disconnected-cluster state.

Each current tunnel owner contributes at most five ordinary samples: two gauges, one reconnect counter and two direction counters. Current owner identity includes the process platform-instance label. A cluster rename removes old gauge-name labels; a disconnect retains its zero gauge and monotonic counters. These bounds apply to this tunnel family; registration, Vault, SSO, SIEM, snapshot and group lifecycles remain under separate characterization. High-scale collector and whole-application overhead measurements remain pending.
